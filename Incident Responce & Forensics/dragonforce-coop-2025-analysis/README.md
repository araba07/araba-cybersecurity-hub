## Co-op 2025 Cyberattack: Incident Response & Risk Analysis
This project is my independent analysis of the 2025 Co-op cyberattack carried out by the DragonForce ransomware group. The goal of this investigation is to examine the incident from the perspective of a cybersecurity analyst, exploring how the attack occurred, the vulnerabilities that enabled it, how Co-op responded, and what lessons can be learned to improve organisational security.

## Why I Chose This Incident

As a Computer Science student with a strong interest in cybersecurity, this incident stood out to me because I witnessed its impact firsthand. During the attack, I visited the Co-op store located on my university campus and immediately noticed that the self-service tills were unavailable and contactless payments were not functioning. At the time, I was unaware of the scale of the cyberattack taking place behind the scenes, but seeing the disruption affect a business in real time was both eye-opening and fascinating.
The experience made me realise that cyberattacks are not just technical events confined to data centres and security teams—they can have a direct impact on everyday operations and thousands of ordinary customers.

## Disclaimer

This project is intended for educational and portfolio purposes only. All analysis is based on publicly available information, media reports, and cybersecurity research published following the incident.
